New in v0.5.0 — the whole plug-in as a cassette

Two looks, one instrument.

Same controls, same sound, same project — only the face changes. Pick it in the ••• menu; it stays on your computer, and Classic stays exactly as it was.

  • The hubs turn with the tape

    Forward, back, faster, slower — and they stand still in FREEZE.

  • The Memory lives in the window

    Press it and the tape pulls out across the label; drag along it to set Focus.

  • Side A, side B

    A is FREE, B is SYNC. The face turns over when you switch.

Catch it after it happens.

FLOATING is always listening. When something good goes by, press Capture: the last few seconds become a Memory you can reshape, place in space and print back into your session. The original stays untouched.
